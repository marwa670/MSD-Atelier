MSD Atelier - Where Casablanca Meets Couture
Founded in Sidi Maarouf, Casablanca, MSD Atelier reimagines everyday elegance. We create easy-to-sew,
affordable luxury: mermaid silhouettes that celebrate the body, detachable wings that set you free, and
denim couture that tells your story. Designed by Marwa Moussaid , sewn in Casa, worn everywhere. This is MARWINGS .
THE HOUSE OF MSD
MSD ATELIER is not a brand. It is a House.
Founded in Casablanca by Marwa Moussaid- a girl from Sidi Maarouf who believed that luxury should not be
a privilege, but a feeling.
In a world of fast fashion, we chose the opposite:
slow design, sharp cuts, and details that fly.
Our signature? The Mermaid Cut — that sculpts the body like second skin.
The MARWINGS — detachable wings that transform one dress into two souls.
Denim Couture — because real luxury is eternal.
Each piece is sketched by hand,
sewn by the artisans of Casa, and finished with the obsession of haute couture. This is not for everyone.
This is for the woman who knows she was born to be seen.
